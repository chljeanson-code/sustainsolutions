MEET THE FOUNDER

My name is Charles-Louis, I demonstrate around 10 years of experience in material and process specialized in plastics, foams and rubbers for Sports. SUSTAIN Solutions was created to help brands, organizations and industrials to turn sustainable and innovatives ideas into practical engineering solutions.

At the core of my work are people, trust, and long-term relationships. After six years living and working in Asia, particularly immersed in Chinese culture, I developed a deep appreciation for multicultural collaboration and adaptability. These experiences reinforced my belief that diversity and strong human connections are powerful drivers of innovation.

Sport has always been a central part of my life. When I am not working on sustainable and innovative technologies, you will probably find me cycling, exploring new trails, or in the water enjoying surfing or wingfoiling. These experiences continuously inspire the way I approach innovation and product design.

Cycling teaches endurance and long-term vision. Being IRON MAN helped me to build a strong resilience along projects.

Water sports inspire resilience, adaptability and humility

Building meaningful connections through collective action and collaboration.

Enjoying the outdoors reinforces my commitment to sustainability.
